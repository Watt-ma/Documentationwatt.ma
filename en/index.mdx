watt.ma is a cloud-based Charge Point Management System (CPMS) developed by watt.ma SARL in Casablanca. The platform enables charging operators, businesses, hotels, shopping centers, and municipalities to monitor, monetize, and analyze their electric vehicle charging stations via the open OCPP 1.6J protocol. Already deployed at Al Akhawayn University (Ifrane) and backed by GreenUp Morocco, watt.ma serves Morocco and francophone Africa.

## How it Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [watt.ma](https://watt.ma) and complete your operator profile. Choose the plan suited to your project: Startup, Production, or Enterprise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your stations">
    Configure your OCPP 1.6J compatible stations (Alfen, ABB, Ratio, Teison, and so on) with the URL and credentials provided by watt.ma.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure pricing">
    Define your charging rates per kWh, per minute, or as flat fees. Activate QR code CMI payments or RFID cards according to your needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor in real time">
    Track the status of your stations, ongoing sessions, revenue, and alerts from the real-time dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
