watt.ma lets you collaborate with your team by assigning roles suited to each member. Whether you are a small property manager or a charge point operator with multiple sites, access management ensures every user has the permissions they need without reaching sensitive data.

<Note>
  The number of administrators is limited by your plan: **3** for Startup, **15** for Production, **unlimited** for Enterprise. Go to **Account \> Plans** to view your current limit.
</Note>

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open team management">
    Sign in to [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma), click your profile in the top right, then select **Account \> Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Click **Invite member**. Enter the person's email address and select the role to assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the invitation">
    Validate the invitation. The member receives an email with a link to join your organization and set their password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roles and permissions

| Role | Description | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Primary administrator | Full access: stations, sessions, pricing, reports, team, billing, API settings |
| **Operator** | Operational manager | Manages stations, views sessions and reports, edits pricing. No access to billing or team management |
| **Viewer** | Observer | Read-only access to dashboard, sessions, and reports. Cannot modify or create |

## Edit or remove a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team list">
    Open **Account \> Team** to view all active members and pending invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the role">
    Click the action menu to the right of the member and select **Change role**. Choose the new role and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a member">
    To remove a member from your organization, click **Remove** in the action menu. This action is immediate and revokes all their access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a member is irreversible. Their access is revoked immediately and they will no longer be able to sign in to your watt.ma organization. Their historical actions remain preserved.
</Warning>
