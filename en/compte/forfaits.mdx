watt.ma offers three plans tailored to every stage of your charging business: from small property managers with a few stations to national operators or manufacturers looking to deploy a white-label solution. All plans include real-time monitoring, OCPP 1.6J protocol support, and email technical support.

<Note>
  watt.ma uses a SaaS model billed per connector (per charging socket), not per meter. A dual-socket station therefore counts as two connectors on your plan.
</Note>

## Startup

The Startup plan is for small operators, property managers, hotels, and residences deploying their first charging stations. It includes the essentials to monitor a modest fleet and accept CMI QR code payments without a large upfront investment.

## Production

The Production plan meets the needs of CPOs (Charge Point Operators) and growing station manufacturers. It unlocks full API access, revenue sharing reports, smart charging to optimize electrical consumption on your sites, and scheduled exports to automate your reporting.

## Enterprise

The Enterprise plan is designed for large charge point operators, car manufacturers, and integrators looking to deploy watt.ma at scale or as a white label. It includes dedicated support with an SLA guarantee, custom integrations, and unlimited stations, RFID cards, and administrators.

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your current plan">
    Sign in to [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma) and open **Account \> Plans** to view your current plan and usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new plan">
    Click **Upgrade** under the plan you want. The price difference is calculated pro rata for the remaining time in your billing period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upgrade">
    Validate the change. New features are activated immediately and the new billing applies starting with the next cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>
