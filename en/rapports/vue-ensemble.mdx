watt.ma gives you a complete set of reports to track your charging station activity and make informed decisions. Whether you are a charge point operator, property manager, or station manufacturer, the data collected through the OCPP 1.6J protocol is transformed into clear, actionable indicators.

## Available reporting modules

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Revenue" icon="chart-line" href="/en/rapports/revenus">
    Track your revenue by period, site, station, and payment method. Access revenue sharing reports based on your plan.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sessions" icon="bolt" href="/en/sessions/suivi-sessions">
    View detailed charging session history: duration, energy delivered, user, and payment status.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Energy" icon="plug">
    Visualize total and site-level energy consumption. Identify most-used stations and demand peaks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Revenue sharing" icon="users">
    Available on the Production and Enterprise plans, this report details the revenue split between site owners and operators.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Access the reports

Sign in to [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma) and open the **Reports** section from the main menu. Each module displays tables that you can filter by period, site, and station. Data updates in real time as sessions complete.

<Tip>
  Use the date filters to compare periods and identify usage trends for your charging stations.
</Tip>
