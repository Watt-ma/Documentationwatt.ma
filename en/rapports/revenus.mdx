title: "Track charging revenue on watt.ma"
sidebarTitle: "Revenue"
description: "Analyze charging revenue by period, site, station, and payment method. Enable revenue sharing reports based on your plan."
---

The watt.ma revenue dashboard lets you precisely track the financial activity generated by your charging stations. Every completed session automatically feeds the report, whether it was paid by CMI QR code, RFID card, or free mode.

## View your revenue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports section">
    Sign in to [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma) and click **Reports** in the side menu, then select **Revenue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by period">
    Choose a preset period (today, this week, this month, this year) or set a custom date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine the analysis">
    Filter by site, individual station, or payment method (CMI QR code, RFID, free) to isolate the data that matters to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the indicators">
    Review total revenue, number of paid sessions, average per session, and payment method breakdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revenue sharing reports

If you operate stations on third-party sites, the revenue sharing report lets you define an automatic split between the site owner and the charging operator.

<Note>
  Revenue sharing reports are available starting with the **Production** plan. The **Startup** plan only shows aggregated revenue without a split.
</Note>

### Enable revenue sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your plan">
    Make sure your account is on the Production or Enterprise plan. Go to **Account \> Plans** to verify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a split">
    In **Reports \> Revenue sharing**, select a site and define the split percentage between the site owner and the operator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the report">
    The table updates automatically after each session. Export data as CSV for your accounting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
