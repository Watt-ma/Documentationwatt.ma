---
title: "Export charging data from watt.ma"
sidebarTitle: "Export"
description: "Export sessions and revenue as CSV, schedule automatic email deliveries, and manage retention of your historical charging data."
---

watt.ma lets you export all your charging data to integrate into your accounting, internal dashboards, or regulatory reporting obligations. Exports cover charging sessions, revenue, and energy consumption data.

## Export manually as CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select data type">
    Sign in to [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma), open the **Reports** section, and select the module you want: Sessions, Revenue, or Energy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Set the analysis period and apply filters by site or station to limit the export to relevant data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the export">
    Click the **Export** button at the top right of the table. The CSV file automatically downloads to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule automatic exports

You can configure periodic report deliveries by email to automate your administrative follow-up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open export settings">
    In the **Reports** section, click **Scheduled exports** in the side menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a rule">
    Click **New scheduled export**. Choose the report type, frequency (daily, weekly, monthly), and email recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and activate">
    Review the settings and activate the rule. The first export will be sent according to the chosen frequency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Scheduled exports are available starting with the **Production** plan. The **Startup** plan only supports manual exports from the interface.
</Note>

## Data retention

<Note>
  watt.ma keeps your complete session and revenue history with no time limit for the Production and Enterprise plans. The **Startup** plan retains data for the last 12 months. Upgrade to a higher plan to unlock access to your full history.
</Note>

## CSV file format

Exported files use CSV format with semicolon separator and UTF-8 encoding. Columns include: session ID, start and end date and time, station, energy delivered in kWh, billed amount, payment method, and transaction status.
