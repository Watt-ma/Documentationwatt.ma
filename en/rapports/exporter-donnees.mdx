watt.ma lets you export all your charging data to integrate it into your accounting, internal dashboards, or regulatory reporting obligations. Exports cover charging sessions, revenue, and energy consumption data.

## Available export types

watt.ma offers a per-business-entity export so you can extract exactly the data you need. Each file contains a unique primary key and join keys to cross-reference tables in your analysis tool or ERP.

| Export | Primary key | Main join keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charging sessions | `session_id` | `charger_id`, `driver_id`, `site_id`, `billing_account_id` |
| Tariffs | `tariff_id` | `site_id`, `charger_id` |
| Customers | `customer_id` | `billing_account_id` |
| Billing accounts | `billing_account_id` | `customer_id` |
| Invoices | `invoice_id` | `billing_account_id`, `customer_id` |
| Invoice lines | `invoice_line_id` | `invoice_id`, `session_id` |
| Payments | `payment_id` | `invoice_id`, `billing_account_id` |
| Failed payments | `payment_id` | `invoice_id` |
| Refunds | `refund_id` | `payment_id`, `invoice_id` |
| Credit notes | `credit_note_id` | `invoice_id`, `billing_account_id` |
| Partner settlements | `settlement_id` | `site_id`, `partner_id` |
| Revenue | Aggregated | `site_id`, `charger_id`, period |
| Energy consumption | Aggregated | `site_id`, `charger_id`, period |

## Export manually in CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the data type">
    Log in to [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma), open the **Reports** section, and select the desired module: Sessions, Revenue, or Energy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Define the analysis period and apply filters by site or charger to limit the export to relevant data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the export">
    Click the **Export** button at the top right of the table. The CSV file downloads automatically to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule automatic exports

You can configure periodic report deliveries by email to automate your administrative follow-up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open export settings">
    In the **Reports** section, click **Scheduled exports** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a rule">
    Click **New scheduled export**. Choose the report type, frequency (daily, weekly, monthly), and email recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate and activate">
    Check the settings and activate the rule. The first export will be sent according to the chosen frequency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Scheduled exports are available from the **Production** plan. The **Startup** plan only allows manual exports from the interface.
</Note>

## Data retention

<Note>
  watt.ma retains the complete history of your sessions and revenue with no time limit for the Production and Enterprise plans. The **Startup** plan retains data for the last 12 months. Upgrade to a higher plan to unlock access to your full history.
</Note>

## CSV file format

Exported files use CSV format with semicolon separator and UTF-8 encoding. Columns include: session identifier, start and end date and time, affected charger, energy delivered in kWh, invoiced amount, payment method, and transaction status.

## Accounting use cases

watt.ma CSV exports and scheduled deliveries address several common accounting and regulatory needs in Morocco:

- **Bank reconciliation**: reconcile CMI and Stripe collections with corresponding invoices by importing the payment list into your accounting software.
- **Transmission to accountant**: regularly send your accountant exports of invoices, invoice lines, and payments for formal bookkeeping in MAD.
- **ERP import**: integrate customer, billing account, and partner settlement files into your ERP via the provided join keys.
- **Tax audit**: produce session, invoice, and payment files on demand for a fiscal inspection.
- **VAT calculation**: export invoices and invoice lines to calculate and verify VAT collected at the 20% rate.
- **Monthly closing**: build each month an aggregated export of revenue and energy consumption for your management reports.
