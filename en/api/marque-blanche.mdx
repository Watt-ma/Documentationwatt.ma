---
title: "Deploy watt.ma as a white-label solution"
sidebarTitle: "White Label"
description: "Customize watt.ma with your logo, colors, and domain. Available on the Enterprise plan; contact the team to request it."
---

The watt.ma white-label option lets you deploy the charging station management platform under your own visual identity. Your customers and end users access the dashboard and payment app through your domain, strengthening your brand consistency.

This option is intended for station manufacturers, growing charge point operators, and integrators who want to offer a turnkey solution to their clients without internal development.

<Note>
  The white-label option is reserved for the **Enterprise** plan. Contact the sales team for a custom quote tailored to your station volume and integration needs.
</Note>

## Available customization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Logo and visual identity">
    Replace the watt.ma logo with your own high-resolution logo. The main interface colors (navigation bar, primary buttons, status indicators) are adapted to your brand guidelines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom domain">
    Configure a subdomain of your choice (for example: `recharge.yourcompany.ma`) or a dedicated root domain. watt.ma manages the SSL certificate and production deployment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Emails and notifications">
    Payment receipts, station alerts, and team invitations are sent with your sender address and signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom integrations">
    The Enterprise plan includes the ability to develop connectors specific to your ecosystem: ERP, CRM, billing system, or proprietary mobile app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Request a white-label deployment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your specification">
    Gather your vector logo, brand guidelines, and list of integration needs. Specify the number of stations involved and the deployment sites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the sales team">
    Send your request to [**contact@wattsc.com**](mailto:contact@wattsc.com) with subject line "White-label request". You can also reach us via WhatsApp at **\+212 770 238 225**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scoping call">
    The watt.ma team offers a scoping call to validate technical specifications, deployment timeline, and contractual terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Once the contract is signed, the technical team sets up your dedicated environment and supports you during testing before production launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The typical lead time for a white-label solution is **4 to 6 weeks** from specification validation. This timeline may vary depending on the complexity of the custom integrations requested.
</Note>
