sidebarTitle: "Profile"

---

The **Profile** screen in the watt.ma app centralizes all actions related to your driver account: wallet balance, payment methods, transaction history, favorite stations, and settings. It is the app's personal hub.

## Overview

<img
  src="/images/app-mobile/07-profile.png"
  alt="Profile screen in the watt.ma app"
  width="320"
/>

At the top: your name (or `Guest` in demo mode), your profile photo, and a card showing your **wallet balance** with a direct **Top Up** button.

## Profile Sections

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Wallet" icon="wallet" href="/en/app-mobile/paiement">
    Balance in dirhams, top-ups, and automatic debits at the end of each session.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Payment Methods" icon="credit-card" href="/en/app-mobile/paiement">
    Saved Visa, Mastercard, and CMI cards, default card, plus adding and removing cards.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Transactions" icon="receipt" href="/en/app-mobile/historique">
    Chronological log of top-ups, sessions, and refunds.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Favorites" icon="heart" href="/en/app-mobile/favoris">
    Pinned stations for quick access from the map.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Settings" icon="gear" href="/en/app-mobile/parametres">
    Notifications, location, dark mode, language, and legal documents.
  </Card>

  <Card title="FAQs" icon="circle-question" href="/en/app-mobile/faq">
    Quick answers to the most common driver questions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Quick Actions

- **Top up the wallet**: green button directly on the balance card.
- **Sign out**: at the bottom of the Settings screen.
- **Delete account**: request it at [contact@wattsc.com](mailto:contact@wattsc.com), in compliance with law 09-08.

<Tip>
  Station operators use a separate interface: the web [CPMS dashboard](/en/bornes/tableau-de-bord).
</Tip>
