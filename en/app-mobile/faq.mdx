## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I start a charging session?">
    Open the **Charge** tab, scan the QR code displayed on the station, and the session starts automatically. The cost is debited from your wallet at the end of the session. Full details on the [Start Charging](/en/app-mobile/demarrer-recharge) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I top up my wallet?">
    From the **Profile** tab, tap **Top Up** above the wallet balance, choose an amount in dirhams (50, 100, 200, 500 MAD or custom), and confirm with your saved Visa, Mastercard, or CMI card. Details on [Wallet and Payment](/en/app-mobile/paiement).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What connector types are supported?">
    Stations on the watt.ma network expose three connector types: **CCS2**, **CHAdeMO**, and **Type 2**. The type is shown on the [Station Details](/en/app-mobile/details-station) screen before you travel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if the station does not start?">
    First check that the connector is fully inserted and that your vehicle accepts charging. If the problem persists, contact support with the station reference: [contact@wattsc.com](mailto:contact@wattsc.com) or WhatsApp `+212 770 238 225`. The station operator can also restart it remotely from the [CPMS](/en/bornes/tableau-de-bord).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get an invoice?">
    Every transaction is recorded in **Profile → Transactions**. PDF export per session is planned in a future release. For an immediate invoice, send the transaction reference to [contact@wattsc.com](mailto:contact@wattsc.com). See the [Roadmap](/roadmap).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
