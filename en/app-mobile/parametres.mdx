The **Settings** screen in the watt.ma app gathers the driver experience preferences and links to legal documents. It opens from **Profile → Settings**.

## Overview

<img
  src="/images/app-mobile/11-settings.png"
  alt="Settings screen in the watt.ma app"
  width="320"
/>

The screen is divided into two sections:

- **Preferences**: everyday usage options.
- **Account**: payment methods and legal documents.

At the bottom of the screen, the installed version is displayed (for example `watt.ma v1.0.0`).

## Preferences

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Enables push notifications (session end, top-up, station alerts) |
| Location services | Allows the app to display the nearest stations on the map |
| Dark mode | Toggles between light and dark theme, independent of system setting |

Notifications use Expo Notifications. Location uses the standard Expo Location API: GPS or map fallback depending on the precision required by the screen.

## Account

- **Payment Methods**: opens card management. See [Wallet and Payment](/en/app-mobile/paiement).
- **FAQs**: frequently asked questions. See [FAQ](/en/app-mobile/faq).
- **Terms & Conditions**: watt.ma terms of use.
- **Privacy Policy**: personal data processing under law 09-08.

## Language

The app is published in French. Multilingual support (Arabic and English) is planned for a future release. See the [Roadmap](/roadmap).

## Sign Out and Delete Account

- **Sign out**: button at the bottom of the Settings screen. Returns to the [sign-in](/en/app-mobile/inscription-connexion) screen.
- **Delete account**: send the request to [contact@wattsc.com](mailto:contact@wattsc.com). Your personal data will be erased in compliance with law 09-08.
