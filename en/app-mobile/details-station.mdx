When you tap a station on the map or in the list, the watt.ma app opens the **Station details** screen. You get everything you need before driving out: live availability, connectors, per-kWh rate, and one-tap GPS routing.

## What you see at the top

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Status badge** | `Open`, `Closed`, or `Under maintenance`, based on hours and OCPP status |
| **Station name** | Commercial name set by the operator (for example `Total Energies Djebilet`) |
| **Address and city** | Full address with city (Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Ifrane, etc.) |
| **Distance** | Estimate in km from your location, if location permission is granted |
| **Rating** | Average driver rating, when available |

## Charge points and connectors

Below the header, three counters summarize the station's capacity:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Charge points" icon="charging-station">
    Total number of physical connectors installed at the site.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Speeds" icon="gauge-high">
    Slow AC, fast AC, and DC fast charging, with the max power in kW.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Connectors" icon="plug">
    Supported plug types: CCS2, CHAdeMO, Type 2.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Under the counters, the **Available connectors** list shows each plug with:

- Connector type and rated power (for example `CCS2 · 60 kW`)
- Live status: `Available`, `Charging`, `Occupied`, `Out of service`
- Applied rate in MAD per kWh
- A colored dot to spot free connectors at a glance

<Tip>
  Green dot = free, orange = charging (freeing up soon), red = out of service. Tap a connector to open the start-charging screen if you are on site.
</Tip>

## Real-time status

Each connector's status is streamed live from the [watt.ma CPMS](/en/bornes/vue-ensemble) over **OCPP 1.6J**. `StatusNotification` events update the app within seconds, no manual refresh required.

<Note>
  If you are outside network coverage, the app shows the last known status with a timestamp. It resyncs as soon as connectivity is back.
</Note>

## Rate and practical info

- **Rate**: MAD per kWh, set by the charger operator in their [pricing profile](/en/sessions/tarifs). Some sites apply peak / off-peak rates.
- **Hours**: public opening hours, with any exceptional closures.
- **Access**: public, reserved for a venue's customers, or private (residential, office building).
- **Amenities**: covered parking, café, restaurant, restrooms, wheelchair accessible.
- **Payment accepted**: CMI (Moroccan bank card), Stripe (international card), watt.ma RFID card.

## Start navigation

The **Start navigation** button opens your phone's default maps app (Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze) with the route preloaded to the station.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Start navigation">
    The app offers the maps app installed on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your navigation app">
    Choose Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, or another compatible app. You can save this choice as default in [Settings](/en/app-mobile/parametres).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the route">
    The optimized route appears with estimated travel time and required kWh.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Status shows Out of service but the charger seems to work">
    The charger may have lost its OCPP connection. The app shows the last status reported by the CPMS. Contact support at \+212 770 238 225 if you are on site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Start navigation button does not open">
    Confirm a maps app (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze) is installed and that you granted watt.ma permission to open other apps in the system settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The displayed rate does not match my invoice">
    Some sites apply peak / off-peak rates or a resident-specific rate. See the [Pricing page](/en/sessions/tarifs) to understand the calculation, or open the session in [History](/en/app-mobile/historique) for the breakdown.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
