The **Favorites** tab in the watt.ma app lets you pin the stations you use most often: home, office, or regular route chargers. This page describes how to add, remove, and find your favorites.

## Overview

<img
  src="/images/app-mobile/06-favorites.png"
  alt="Favorites tab in the watt.ma app"
  width="320"
/>

The **Favorites** screen shows the number of pinned stations and the corresponding list. When no station has been added yet, the app displays an empty state with a helpful message.

## Add a Station to Favorites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the station">
    From the map or list, tap the station you want to pin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the heart">
    At the top right of the [Station Details](/en/app-mobile/details-station) screen, tap the heart icon to activate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the station">
    Open the **Favorites** tab at the bottom of the app: the station appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a Favorite

From the **Favorites** tab or the station details screen, tap the heart icon again to deactivate it. The station remains accessible via the map and search.

## Synchronization

- Favorites are linked to your watt.ma account and **synced** across your devices as soon as you sign in with the same account.
- Storage is on the FastAPI backend, in MongoDB.

<Tip>
  Combine favorites with the [history](/en/app-mobile/historique) to identify your most reliable stations at the start and end of each trip.
</Tip>
