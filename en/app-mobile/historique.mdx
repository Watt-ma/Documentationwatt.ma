The **Profile** tab in the watt.ma app centralizes your balance, preferences, and help resources. This page describes every screen available for tracking your activity and customizing your experience.

## Profile Hub

The main profile screen displays:

- Your **wallet balance** in dirhams (MAD)
- **Quick access** to wallet, payment methods, and history
- Your account information (name, validated phone)

## Transaction History

The chronological log groups:

- **Top-ups** to your wallet with date and amount
- **Debits** from completed sessions with the energy delivered in kWh, duration, and station

Use filters to isolate a period or an operation type.

## App Settings

The **Settings** screen lets you adjust:

- **Push notifications** (session start/stop, successful top-up, low-balance alerts)
- **Dark mode**
- Language preferences (French at launch, Arabic and English planned, see the [roadmap](/roadmap))

## Built-in FAQ

The **Frequently Asked Questions** screen offers in-app help for Moroccan drivers: city coverage, how to add a CMI card, how Mock Mode works, what to do if a QR scan fails, etc.

## Upcoming Features

According to the project roadmap:

- Full OCPP roaming client for remote charging commands (start/stop) on third-party networks
- Connection to a Moroccan SMS gateway for production OTP
- Native CMI SDK integration for Moroccan cards
- Multilingual support (Arabic, French, English)
- Automatic PDF invoice export per completed session
