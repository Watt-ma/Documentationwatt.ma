---

The watt.ma app displays an interactive map and list of charging stations in Morocco's main cities: Casablanca, Marrakech, Rabat, Tangier, and beyond. Each station is enriched with operational details to help you choose the right charging point before you drive.

<img
  src="/images/app-mobile/04-map.png"
  alt="Station Map screen in the watt.ma app"
  width="320"
/>

## Map View and List View

The **Stations** tab offers two synchronized views:

- **Map**: stations displayed on a map centered on your location or the selected city.
- **List**: stations sorted by proximity, with a quick availability preview.

You can switch between the two views at any time without losing your filters.

## Station Details

When you select a station, you see:

- The **connectors** available and their type
- The **power** rating (43 kW by default, typical of semi-fast and fast urban Moroccan chargers)
- The **pricing** applied in dirhams (MAD)
- The **amenities** at the host site (parking, cafe, WiFi, etc.)

## Favorites

Stations you use regularly can be added to **Favorites**. The **Favorites** tab then lets you return to them in one tap from any screen, without going back through the map.

<Tip>
  Add your home, work, and frequent route stations to favorites to start a session more quickly.
</Tip>

## Search by City

Use the search bar to filter results by Moroccan city (Casablanca, Marrakech, Rabat, Tangier, etc.). The list and map update live as you type.
