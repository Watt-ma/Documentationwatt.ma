watt.ma gives you centralized management of your entire charging station fleet, whether you operate a single site or a nationwide multi-site deployment. From a single interface, you monitor each station, each connector, and each session in real time.

## Key Concepts

Before connecting your first stations, familiarize yourself with the three pillars of management on watt.ma:

- **Site**: a physical location (parking lot, hotel, residence) that groups one or more charging stations.
- **Station**: the physical charging point, identified by a serial number and model.
- **Connector**: the individual socket on a station (Type 2, CHAdeMO, CCS, and so on). It is at the connector level that watt.ma checks availability and starts sessions.

## Hardware Compatibility

watt.ma is built on the open **OCPP 1.6J** (Open Charge Point Protocol) standard. Any station compatible with this protocol can be connected without complex technical intervention. Brands already validated by our team and customers include:

- Alfen
- ABB
- Ratio
- Teison

<Note>
  If your station supports OCPP 1.6J but is not on this list, it will very likely work with watt.ma. Contact us on WhatsApp at **\+212 770 238 225** for a quick check.
</Note>
