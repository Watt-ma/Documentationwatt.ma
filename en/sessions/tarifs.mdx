The watt.ma pricing engine lets you build each charging price from multiple components: energy, connection fees, time, idle fees, and discounts. You can charge per kilowatthour, per hour of connection, or offer fixed-rate packages, then assign these tariffs at the level of a site, a charger, or a user group. This page guides you through creating a complete tariff.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new tariff">
    In the sidebar, go to **Sessions \> Tariffs**, then click **New tariff**. Enter a recognizable name (for example, "Hotel guest tariff" or "Residence off-peak").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a pricing mode">
    Select one of the three modes:

    - **Per kWh**: price per kilowatthour consumed, the most common for public charging
    - **Per hour**: fixed price per hour of connection, suitable for long-term parking
    - **Flat rate**: fixed amount per session, regardless of duration or energy
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure differentiated pricing (optional)">
    Enable the **Off-peak / peak hours** option to apply two different prices depending on the time period. Define the corresponding time windows. This feature is useful for encouraging charging during periods of low electricity demand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the tariff to chargers or sites">
    Once the tariff is saved, click **Assign**. Select the sites or specific chargers this tariff applies to. You can associate several tariffs with different contexts (public, residential, fleet).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing components

Each tariff is a sum of components that you activate or leave inactive depending on your business model:

| Component | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Energy price** | Amount per kWh delivered during the charge. |
| **Connection fee** | Fixed amount applied once, at the start of the session. |
| **Time pricing** | Amount based on the charge duration, connection duration, or parking duration. |
| **Off-peak / peak hours** | Differentiated prices based on configurable time windows. |
| **Idle fees** | Amount charged after a grace period once the charge ends. |
| **Discounts and groups** | Reductions applied based on the driver group (residents, employees, fleet). |
| **Price cap** | Maximum limit on the total charge or the unit price. |
| **Monthly subscription** | Recurring flat fee covering part or all of the charging sessions. |

To explore each component in detail, see the [Pricing components](/en/sessions/composants-tarifaires) page.

## Tariff priority

When several tariffs could apply to the same session, watt.ma follows a deterministic hierarchy from the most specific to the most general. For example, a tariff attached to a connector takes priority over a tariff attached to the entire site. If you configure overlapping tariffs, it is advisable to understand this logic before going live.

To learn more, see [Tariff priority](/en/sessions/priorite-tarifs).

<Note>
  Amounts shown in watt.ma are expressed in Moroccan dirhams (MAD). The applicable VAT in Morocco for electric charging services is 20%. You must configure the VAT rate in your account billing settings so that receipts and reports reflect the correct amounts including tax.
</Note>
