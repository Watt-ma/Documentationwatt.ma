---
title: "Accept QR Code Payments with CMI on watt.ma"
sidebarTitle: "CMI Payments"
description: "Activate QR code CMI payments on watt.ma: users pay for charging sessions by scanning a code from their Moroccan banking app."
---

watt.ma natively integrates QR code payments through the Centre Monétique Interbancaire (CMI). This feature allows your users to pay for their charging sessions by scanning a QR code displayed on the station screen or their phone, directly from their compatible Moroccan banking app. No physical payment terminal is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your CMI prerequisites">
    Make sure you have an active **CMI merchant account**. This account is opened through your bank in Morocco and gives you access to electronic payment acceptance via the CMI network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate CMI integration in watt.ma">
    Sign in to [watt.ma](https://watt.ma), then go to **Settings \> Payments**. Click **Activate CMI**, then enter the merchant credentials provided by your bank (contract number, API key, or certificate depending on the format provided by CMI).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure stations for QR display">
    In each compatible station's settings, enable the **Display CMI QR code on screen** option. When a user plugs in their vehicle, the station displays a unique QR code linked to the current session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a complete payment">
    Launch a test session from a station. Scan the QR code with a Moroccan banking app (for example, your bank's CMI partner app). Verify that the payment is triggered, the session starts automatically, and the receipt is generated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  CMI integration requires an active merchant account with a Moroccan bank that is a CMI member. Without this account, the QR code payment option remains inactive in the watt.ma interface.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Enable the **WhatsApp receipt** option in notification settings. After each successful CMI payment, the user automatically receives a detailed receipt via WhatsApp with the amount, duration, energy consumed, and location of the charge. This feature is available starting from the Startup plan.
</Tip>
