The interactive map [**map.watt.ma**](https://map.watt.ma) is the public reference for EV charging stations in Morocco. It brings together in a single place the chargers managed by the watt.ma CPMS, partner networks interconnected through the OCPI protocol, and stations verified by the driver community.

## Three sources of chargers in one map

map.watt.ma aggregates three distinct sources to offer the most complete coverage of the Moroccan territory.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="watt.ma network" icon="bolt">

  <Card title="OCPI partner networks" icon="arrows-rotate">

### watt.ma network chargers

Every charger supervised by the watt.ma CPMS appears on the map automatically. Status is updated live over OCPP 1.6J:

- **Available** (green): ready to start a session
- **In use** (orange): session in progress
- **Out of service** (gray): disconnected or faulty

Tariffs shown on the map come directly from the rules configured on [web.watt.ma](https://web.watt.ma). See [Tariffs](/en/sessions/tarifs) for supported pricing components.

### Partner networks over OCPI

watt.ma implements the **OCPI** (Open Charge Point Interface) protocol to interconnect its network with other Moroccan charge point operators. A partner charger connected through OCPI appears on map.watt.ma with:

- Location and GPS coordinates
- Synced availability status
- Public tariffs from the operator
- Connector types and power ratings
- Operator name of origin

<Note>
  OCPI rollout is underway. See the [roadmap](/en/roadmap) for the progress of interconnection agreements and the list of available partner networks.
</Note>

### Community-identified chargers

Some charging stations installed in Morocco are not yet connected to a CPMS or an OCPI network. Drivers can report them on map.watt.ma to help others locate them.

Each community charger is marked distinctly and enriched with:

- Address and photo reported by a driver
- Observed connector type
- Confirmation from other drivers that it works
- Reviews and ratings after use
- Outage or unavailability reports

<Tip>
  Do you know a charger in Morocco that is missing from the map? Add it from map.watt.ma to help other drivers and grow national coverage.
</Tip>

## Main features

### Advanced filtering

- **Source**: watt.ma network, OCPI partners, community
- **Availability**: show only free chargers
- **Connector type**: Type 2, CHAdeMO, CCS Combo, domestic
- **Power**: slow, accelerated, fast, ultra-fast
- **Pricing**: filter by kWh price range
- **Operator**: show chargers from a specific operator

### Station details

Click a marker to open the full information: address, available connectors, tariffs, opening hours, user reviews, nearby amenities and origin operator.

### Route planning

Built-in GPS integration draws a route to the chosen station and suggests stops based on remaining range and tariffs.

## Access to map.watt.ma

The map is **public and free**. No account is required to browse the network. To manage favorites and report community chargers, sign in with your watt.ma account.

## Data freshness

| Data | Update frequency | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| watt.ma charger status | Real time (OCPP 1.6J) | watt.ma CPMS |
| Partner charger status | OCPI sync | Origin operator |
| Community chargers | On validation | Drivers and cross-checks |
| Tariffs | On change | Origin operator |
| User reviews | Continuous | Community |

<Note>
  Information comes from operators and the community. If you see incorrect data, report it from the station detail page.
</Note>
