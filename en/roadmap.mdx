## In Progress

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Roaming and OCPI Integration" icon="arrows-rotate">
    **Highest priority.** Transparent inter-network charging with full OCPI protocol support for roaming agreements.

    - OCPI 2.2.1 protocol implementation
    - Hubject and ENAPI integration
    - Automated roaming agreements
    - Inter-network session management
    - Real-time CDR exchange
  </Card>

  <Card title="OCPP 2.x Support" icon="bolt">
    **High priority.** Full compatibility with the latest OCPP standard for next-generation stations.

    - Full OCPP 2.x message support
    - Device management profiles
    - Compatible with ISO 15118 Plug & Charge
    - Enhanced security profiles
    - Backward compatibility with 1.6
  </Card>

  <Card title="Dynamic Smart Charging" icon="gauge-high">
    **Normal priority.** Reactive network load management with real-time energy price optimization.

    - Real-time load balancing
    - Energy price optimization
    - Network integration APIs
    - V2G preparation
    - Demand response support
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Planned

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Mobile Applications" icon="mobile-screen">
    **Medium priority.** Native iOS and Android apps for drivers and operators, with all platform features.

    - Native iOS and Android apps
    - Real-time station status
    - In-app integrated payments
    - Push notifications
    - Management tools for operators
  </Card>

  <Card title="Integrated Native Billing" icon="file-invoice">
    **Normal priority.** Embedded payment solutions, automated invoicing, and revenue management tools.

    - Stripe and PayPal integration
    - Automated invoicing
    - Subscription management
    - Revenue analysis
    - Multi-currency support
  </Card>

  <Card title="White Label" icon="palette">
    **Normal priority.** Full platform customization for charging operators and resellers.

    - Custom branding and themes
    - White-label mobile apps
    - Custom domain support
    - Branded driver experience
    - Reseller management tools
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
