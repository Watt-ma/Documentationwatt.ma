---

Before you start monitoring energy across your facilities, complete your account setup. This ensures accurate cost tracking, correct local timestamps, and secure team access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Go to **Account \> Profile** and fill in your full name, phone number, and company details. Upload a company logo if you want it to appear on exported reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Navigate to **Account \> Team Management** and click **Invite Member**. Enter each colleague's email and assign a role: Admin, Manager, or Viewer. Invited users receive an email to join your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your facility timezone">
    Open **Account \> Facilities**, select a facility, and choose the correct timezone from the dropdown. This aligns all timestamps in dashboards and alerts with local time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your energy tariff">
    In the same facility settings, enter your electricity tariff per kWh. Wattsc uses this rate to calculate estimated costs in consumption reports and analytics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only users with the **Admin** role can change facility settings, tariffs, and billing details. Managers and Viewers have read access to dashboards and reports but cannot modify account-level configuration.
</Note>

Once these steps are complete, your team can begin connecting meters and exploring energy data with accurate local time and cost estimates.
