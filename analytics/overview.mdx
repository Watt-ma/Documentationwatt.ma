## What you can do with analytics

- **Consumption trends**: View usage patterns across hours, days, and months to identify peak demand windows.
- **Benchmarking**: Compare multiple meters, zones, or facilities side by side to find outliers.
- **Cost analysis**: Map consumption to your utility tariff and estimate monthly bills before they arrive.
- **Report scheduling**: Save report configurations and have watt.ma email them to your team weekly or monthly.

## Getting started

If you have not connected any meters yet, start with [Connect Your Meters](/monitoring/connect-meters). Once data is flowing, open the **Analytics** tab from the main navigation to begin exploring reports.
