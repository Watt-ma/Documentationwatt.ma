watt.ma is designed for teams. You can invite colleagues to view or manage meters, assign role-based permissions, and revoke access when needed. This guide explains how to add members and what each role can do.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner, then select **Team Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a member">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter the person's email address, and choose a role from the dropdown. Click **Send Invite**. The recipient will receive an email with a link to join your watt.ma organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign or change roles">
    From the team list, click the role label next to any member to open the role selector. Changes take effect immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a member">
    Click the three-dot menu next to a member and select **Remove**. Their access is revoked immediately, but any reports or alerts they created remain in the account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Role permissions

| Permission | Admin | Manager | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View dashboards and reports | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create and edit alerts | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add or remove meters | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite and manage team members | Yes | No | No |
| Manage billing and plan | Yes | No | No |
| Access API keys | Yes | No | No |

## Best practices

- Give **Admin** only to people who manage billing and account settings.
- Use **Manager** for facility or energy managers who need to configure meters and alerts.
- Use **Viewer** for executives or finance staff who only need read-only access to reports.
