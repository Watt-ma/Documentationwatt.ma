Wattsc offers two plans designed for different facility sizes and data needs. This page compares the Starter and Pro plans, explains how to upgrade or downgrade, and shows you where to find invoices and manage payment methods.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connected meters | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| Data history | 12 months | 36 months |
| Team members | 2 | Unlimited |
| API access | No | Yes |
| Scheduled exports | No | Yes |
| Webhook alerts | No | Yes |
| Price | 299 MAD / month | 999 MAD / month |

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Click your profile avatar, then select **Billing & Plans**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Review the comparison table and click **Upgrade to Pro** (or **Downgrade to Starter** if you are on Pro).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Enter or confirm your payment method, then click **Confirm**. The change takes effect immediately. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invoices and payment history

From the **Billing & Plans** page, click **Invoices** to view and download past invoices as PDFs. You can also update your billing email and company details here.

## Cancellation

To cancel your subscription, open **Billing & Plans**, scroll to the bottom, and click **Cancel Subscription**. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted.

<Note>
  Annual billing saves you two months. When you switch to yearly payment, the discount is applied immediately and your next invoice covers the full 12-month period.
</Note>
